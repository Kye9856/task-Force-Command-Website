Briefing

Everything before you request access

Origin

Task Force Reaper started with a handful of Reforger players who were done with disorganized public lobbies — no comms discipline, no plan, no accountability when things fell apart.

We built TFR to run operations the way they're supposed to run: a real command structure, a real training pipeline, and operators who actually show up. No randoms, no dead weight, no ghost roster.

Why Reforger, only Reforger

We're a single-game unit by design.

Reforger's ballistics, logistics, and scale reward planning over reflexes — the unit that communicates and holds formation wins, not the squad with the fastest trigger fingers. We'd rather be excellent at one platform than mediocre across five.

What we're not

  • A public server with a Discord bolted on
  • A community you join once and never hear from again
  • Interested in operators who show up monthly and call it active duty
  • Built around any game other than Arma Reforger
SOPs

TASK FORCE REAPER: RULES OF SERVICE

Respect Everyone

Treat all members with respect. Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or excessive toxicity will not be tolerated.

Follow Staff Instructions

Listen to staff and leadership during operations and in the Discord. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it respectfully after the mission.

Keep Communications Professional

During operations, maintain radio discipline. Avoid unnecessary chatter when mission-critical communications are taking place.

No Griefing or Teamkilling

Intentional teamkilling, trolling, sabotaging missions, or disrupting gameplay is strictly prohibited.

Use Appropriate Channels

Post content in the correct Discord channels to keep the server organized.

No Spam

Avoid excessive pinging, spam, or posting the same content repeatedly.

Keep Content Appropriate

Do not post illegal content, NSFW material, or excessively graphic real-world violence. Memes and combat-related discussions are welcome as long as they follow Discord’s Terms of Service and remain respectful.

Promotions Are Earned

Ranks are based on activity, teamwork, leadership, and maturity. Do not ask for promotions.

Attend Operations Prepared

Join operations on time, bring the required mods (if applicable), and follow mission briefings.

No Cheating or Exploits

The use of cheats, exploits, or unauthorized modifications is strictly forbidden.

Represent Task Force Reaper Well

Whether in-game or in Discord, act professionally and respectfully. Your behavior reflects on the community.

Have Fun

We’re here to enjoy Arma Reforger together. Take missions seriously when required, but remember that this is a community built for teamwork and fun.

SIGNED, Task Force Reaper Command

Selection

Getting in is a process, not a click

Here's exactly what it looks like, start to finish.

Minimum requirements

17 years or older
Own Arma Reforger
Working microphone and reliable internet
Active Discord account
Can commit to at least 2 operations a month
Willing to learn comms and movement basics — no milsim experience required

The process

1

Submit

Fill out the enlistment form or drop into our Discord and introduce yourself.

2

Interview

A short voice chat with command staff. We're checking fit, not grilling you.

3

Basic Training

One session covering comms discipline, movement, and safety basics.

4

Probation

Attend operations as a probationary operator while leadership gets to know how you work.

5

Full Operator

Roster vote, patch awarded, full access to the command structure.
